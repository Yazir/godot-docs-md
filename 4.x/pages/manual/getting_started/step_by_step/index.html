Step by step

This series builds upon the ref and will get you started with the editor and the engine. You will learn more about nodes and scenes, code your first classes with GDScript, use signals to make nodes communicate with one another, and more.

The following lessons are here to prepare you for doc_your_first_2d_game, a step-by-step tutorial where you will code a game from scratch. By the end of it, you will have the necessary foundations to explore more features in other sections. We also included links to pages that cover a given topic in-depth where appropriate.
